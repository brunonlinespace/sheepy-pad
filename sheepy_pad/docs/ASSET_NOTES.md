# Sheepy Pad 0.4.2 — Application Asset

The official master is `assets/icons/sheepy-pad-master.png`.

It is the approved black spiral notebook with the smiling sheep integrated into the word `sheepy`: `sh` appears to the left, the two `e` characters form the sheep's eyes, and `py` appears to the right.

For 0.2.0 the outer background surrounding the notebook has been removed. The master uses transparency outside the notebook and must not be replaced by a version with an external vignette, glow, halo or backdrop.

The 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 and 512 pixel assets are derived from this master. The AppImage root icon uses the 512 pixel derivative.
